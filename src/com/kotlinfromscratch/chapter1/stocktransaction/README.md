# Stock Transaction

**Source:** Programming Logic and Design, Mr. Amer El-Hashemite, lecture notes, Year 1 Semester 1, Chapter 1, Exercise 11

## Problem
Joe buys 1,000 shares at $32.87 each and pays his broker a 2 percent
commission on the purchase amount. Later he sells all 1,000 shares at
$33.92 each and pays another 2 percent commission, this time on the sale
amount.

Display the amount paid for the stock, the commission on the purchase,
the amount received for the stock, the commission on the sale, and
whether Joe made a profit or took a loss, with the amount.

## Sample run
```
AMOUNT PAID FOR STOCK: 32870.00
AMOUNT PAID TO BROKER AT PURCHASE: 657.40
STOCK SOLD FOR: 33920.00
AMOUNT PAID TO BROKER AT SALE: 678.40
LOSS: 285.80
```
